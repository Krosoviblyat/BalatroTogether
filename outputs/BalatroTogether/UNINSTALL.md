# Uninstall

1. Leave the Together table and close Balatro.
2. Move `%AppData%\Balatro\Mods\BalatroTogether` to a backup location outside Mods, or delete that mod folder if you do not need it. Remove any duplicate Together copy as well.
3. Launch the game. Other mods and normal saves remain independent. Do not delete `%AppData%\Balatro\1` or other profile folders.

If removing all mods, move the Steamodded folder out of Mods and remove the Lovely **winmm.dll** you installed beside Balatro.exe. Only remove a DLL you identified as Lovely; do not remove the game's original libraries. Keep Lovely and Steamodded if other mods need them.

Multiplayer continuation across restarts is not supported. Your normal save may be replaced by a seeded cooperative run when starting one; back up `%AppData%\Balatro` before play if you want to preserve an unfinished single-player run. The development lab used separate save identities; regular player installation uses the normal game's save location.
