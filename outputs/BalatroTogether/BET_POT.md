# Bet Pots

Use **F8 -> Bet Pot** or the Bet Pot HUD button. Choose the **TEAM / +37.5%** or **PERSONAL / +15%** tab. These are fictional Balatro dollars from the current run.

1. Stay unready between blinds or in the native shop. Click the amount field marked **TYPE AMOUNT** and type an exact whole-dollar amount, or use the presets and +/- buttons. Clicking the field selects its current value for replacement. Ctrl+A selects all, Ctrl+V pastes, and Backspace edits. Enter finishes editing without placing a bet. Click **Bet** to confirm. The button stays disabled for zero, more than $10,000, or an amount above your wallet. Dollars leave your own wallet immediately; committed bets cannot be cancelled.
2. Bets apply to the next shared blind. A win saves the stake plus the displayed percentage, rounded down. A loss burns only that blind's bets; earlier saved winnings remain safe.
3. **Team:** pooled bets earn 37.5%, a 50% increase over the old 25% bonus. Only the host can Split Winnings. Each connected, non-retired player, including bots, receives the same whole-dollar amount; any remainder stays in the bank.
4. **Personal:** your bets earn 15%. Only you can Collect My Winnings, and only your own saved bank is paid. Your personal ledger is hidden from other clients. Collecting it never takes from the team pot or withdraws your next blind's bets.

Example: two team bets of $8 save $16 + floor($16 x 0.375) = **$22** after a win. The host can split **$11 each** between two connected players. A personal bet of $20 independently saves **$23**, collectible only by its owner.

Deposits and allocated payouts use ordered run-scoped actions and receipts. Retries cannot repeat a debit or native payout. Pending delivery holds readying and native spending until it settles. Allocated shares stay assigned to their original seats during temporary disconnects; keep the original game open to reconnect. Disconnected seats receive no new team allocation. Retiring a seat abandons undelivered allocated money.

Fast Restart, leaving the table or closing the game abandons both old-run pots. Endless retains them within the same run. There is no persistent bank or closed-process recovery. Bots receive team splits but do not wager automatically. Native wallet reporting is trusted in private co-op.

Limits across both scopes: whole-dollar bets of $1-$10,000 and 4,096 deposit attempts per seat per run. Team and each personal ledger accept at most $800,000 in run deposits. Rejected deposits refund the native wallet.

The screen adds spring entrances, raised pixel borders, button lift/squish, counted winnings, curved coin flights and native sounds. Reduced Motion or intensity zero removes movement. Celebration Effects controls flying coins; Notification Sounds controls added UI sounds. Balatro's money sound follows its native settings.
