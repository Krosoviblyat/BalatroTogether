## What's in the mod?

### Multiplayer & Co-op
- **Up to 4 players** working together to beat Blinds and build absolutely ridiculous decks.
- **Steam integration!** Host a lobby, invite friends through Steam, or join using a six-digit table code.
- **Shared team scoring**, where everyone's hands contribute toward beating the Blind.
- **Adjustable difficulty scaling**, so you can make things easier or much, much worse depending on how many people are playing.
- **Individual decks, Jokers, money, and shops.** Everyone gets to build their own setup while working toward the same goal.
- **Teammate inspection**, so you can keep track of what your friends are working with.
- **AI companions!** You can fill empty seats with bots that actually play hands, shop, discard, and contribute to the run.
- **Blind skip voting**, because sometimes nobody wants to deal with another Small Blind.

### Donation Box
- Donate Jokers and consumables to your friends!
- Send items to specific teammates or leave them available for everyone.
- Supports vanilla and compatible Cryptid cards.
- Card editions and supported ability values carry over when donated.

Got a Joker your friend desperately needs for their cancerous build? Give it to them.

### Betting & Money
I may have gone a little overboard with the gambling systems. But its fine... enjoy it.

- **Team Pot:** Bet money together and earn a 37.5% bonus after winning the next Blind. Only the host can split the winnings among the team.
- **Personal Pot:** Bet your own money for a 15% bonus and collect your winnings whenever they're available.
- Both bonuses round down to whole dollars.

### Reward Dice
Every two won team Blinds, everyone gets a free D6 and D20!

- **Gold D6:** Earn $5 for every number rolled, meaning anywhere from $5 to $30.
- **Red D20:** Gain +1 to +20 Mult on every hand during your next Blind, based on your roll.
- Dice can be saved for later, and your friends can watch you roll them.

And yes, the dice actually roll around in a little physics-based tray. It was awkward to make it work but worth it.

### Boss Reward Wheel
Every defeated Boss Blind gives everyone their own spin on the reward wheel!

There are **21 possible prize sectors**, including rewards like:

- Extra hands and discards for the next Blind.
- Bonus Chips and Mult.
- Free shop rerolls.
- Money, because who doesn't want more money?
- Permanent poker hand level upgrades for the current run.
- Some extremely powerful jackpot rewards.

**And then there's a QTE for a jackpot.**

Instead of spinning normally, you can choose to beat the absolute shit out of the table.

Mash SPACE **32 times in 5 seconds** and you're guaranteed the Perfect Hundred jackpot.

Failing just gives you a normal wheel spin instead.

I don't know why I added this either, but it's staying.

### Custom Bosses
I've started adding my own bosses, and I plan on making a lot more.

**The Harbinger** is one of them!

Unlike ordinary Boss Blinds, The Harbinger actually fights back. It has its own deck, draws cards, plays five-card poker hands, and scores using Chips and Mult just like the players.

Its scored hands damage your team's accumulated Chips, meaning you're not just fighting a score requirement anymore.

There are also boss rules and difficulty options to mess around with.

### Checkpoints & Saved Runs
Because losing an entire multiplayer run to someone's internet connection would be pretty miserable or just a random bug lmao.

- The host can manually save a checkpoint between Blinds.
- **Save & Leave** lets everyone come back later.
- Saved runs preserve player builds, money, Jokers, hand levels, and supported co-op resources.
- The original players can rejoin their saved seats and continue together.
- Multiple parties can have their own saved runs.

These are manual checkpoints, not automatic saves after every hand.

### NEW: Government Man!
My newest custom Legendary Joker, complete with his own artwork!

**Government Man**
- Legendary Joker, $20 base cost.
- Starts at **X2 Mult**.
- Gains **X0.5 Mult** whenever you acquire a new Negative Joker.
- Every second Negative Joker acquired turns a random non-Negative Joker you own into a Negative Joker.
- Appears through the normal Legendary pool, including The Soul.

He's basically a government official who benefits from everyone else's questionable investments.

And yes, I made him look like Jimbo in a suit.

## Installing Balatro Together

I've also made a custom installer and launcher so you don't have to manually install a bunch of things.

The installer includes the compatible versions of:

- Lovely
- Steamodded
- Cryptid
- Amulet
- Balatro Together

**How to install:**

1. Make sure you own Balatro on Steam and have it installed on Windows.
2. Close Balatro completely.
3. Download the permanent **[BalatroTogether.exe](https://github.com/Krosoviblyat/BalatroTogether/releases/download/launcher/BalatroTogether.exe)** installer / launcher.
4. Run the installer and follow the instructions.
5. Launch the game normally through steam, the play together shortcut is a backup.
6. Invite your friends and enjoy!

The same permanent launcher checks GitHub every time it opens and before Play. Future releases use signed update ZIPs, so you do not need a new installer for each patch.

**IMPORTANT:** Everyone in your party needs to be on the same gameplay version, with compatible Cryptid settings. I recommend madness because... funny-
